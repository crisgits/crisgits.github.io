💻 Before You Begin

These are the typical conditions under which Power-Director runs comfortably. Checking them first saves time and avoids frustration halfway through a project.

  • Operating system: Windows 11 or Windows 10, 64-bit version.
  • Processor: A modern multi-core processor from the last several years.
  • Memory: 8 GB of RAM or more is recommended for smooth editing.
  • Storage: Several gigabytes of free space on your main drive for the program and your project files.
  • Display: A screen resolution of 1280 × 720 or higher.
  • Connection: Internet access for the initial download and for any optional extras.

If your computer is a little older, the software will still open and run, but larger projects with high-resolution footage may feel slower. Closing background programs and working with shorter clips helps a great deal.

🎯 Your First Project

Once Power-Director is running, the main window is organized around three simple areas: a media library on the left, a preview window at the top, and a timeline along the bottom. That is all you need to remember to get started.

Bring in your footage

Click the import button, then browse to the folder where your videos and photos live. Select the files you want and confirm. They appear as small thumbnails in the media library.

Place clips on the timeline

Drag a thumbnail from the library down onto the timeline. Repeat for each clip. You can drag clips left or right to change their order, and drag their edges to shorten or lengthen them.

Add polish

Open the effects panel to apply motion effects, transitions, and titles. A preview plays automatically so you can see the result before committing to it.

Save and export

Use the save option to keep your project file, then choose export to produce a finished video you can share, upload, or burn to disc.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The download will not start

Refresh the page, then try again. If a browser pop-up blocker is active, allow pop-ups for the page and retry. Switching to a different browser often solves the problem in seconds.

Windows shows a security prompt

This is normal for software you have just downloaded. Click the option that lets the app run. If the prompt appears again later, choose the same option.

The application is slow or stutters

Close other open programs, especially browsers with many tabs. Lower the preview quality inside the app while you edit, then export at full quality at the end.

Video files do not appear in the library

Check that the files are not inside a compressed archive and that they are stored in a folder your user account can read. Copying them to your Videos folder usually resolves this.

Nothing happens when you double-click

Right-click the file and choose the option to run it as a user with standard permissions. If the file is still downloading, wait until it reaches 100 percent.
